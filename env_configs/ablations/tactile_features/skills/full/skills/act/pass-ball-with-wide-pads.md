## How

A ball does not fit through a gap narrower than itself. Close wider than the
ball you have just felt, step the pads past it, and close again for the next
measurement. The pads then leave the ball where it is instead of carrying it
along.

## Traps

- **A touch that ends in free space is not a ball that is gone.** At a narrow
  gap the pads push the ball for tens of millimetres before it rides up and
  they climb over it; the free reading afterwards is the far plane, exactly as
  if there had been nothing. Read again a little further on rather than
  concluding the row is empty.
- The push is not free. A nudged ball keeps rolling after the pads have left
  it, tens of millimetres in any direction, and shoves whatever is in front of
  it — so a ball measured further along the row may not be the one you were
  aiming for.
- The same narrow gap leaves the imprint at the edge of the pad, so the
  material call taken there under-reads ([[texture-in-relief]]).
- Opening for the walk costs a close on the next site. Budget for it: the
  sweep that never opens is the one that measures one ball and skips the rest.
