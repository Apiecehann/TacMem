## How

At a bounded position-controlled seat, read both raw fingertip views and depth maps. Consider whether the visible patch covers the surface rather than only its edge; retain observations after a stopped motion if the environment remains active.

## Traps

A control refusal does not itself classify the ball. Avoid crushing or assuming that a weakly visible patch means a smooth surface.
