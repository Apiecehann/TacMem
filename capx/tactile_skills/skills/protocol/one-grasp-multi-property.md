## How

Acquire the raw fingertip signals during a gentle grasp and after lifting. If further squeezing is attempted, retain the earlier observations and keep the action stages distinct.

## Traps

A later contact configuration does not replace the earlier unloaded observation. Do not assume a squeeze is reversible.
